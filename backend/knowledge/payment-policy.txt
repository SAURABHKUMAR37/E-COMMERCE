Q: Which payment methods do you accept?
A: You can pay with Cash on Delivery (COD) or online with Razorpay. You choose the payment method on the Place Order page.

Q: Is Cash on Delivery available?
A: Yes. You pay when the order is delivered.

Q: What can I use to pay online?
A: Online payments are made through Razorpay.

Q: Is online payment safe?
A: Online payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card details.

Q: My payment failed. What should I do?
A: Please try again. If money was deducted from your account, contact support at admin@forever.com with your order details.
